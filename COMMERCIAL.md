# Commercial Licensing, Certification & Partnerships

ĀML™ is designed to be broadly implementable while preserving a controlled official brand and commercial ecosystem.

## Open-source software

The software covered by `LICENSE` is available under the MIT License. You do not need to pay ĀRU Intelligence Inc.™ merely to exercise rights granted by that software license.

## What may require a separate agreement

A separate written agreement may be appropriate for uses involving official branding, certification, sponsorship, managed services, enterprise support, OEM/co-branding, or other commercial relationships, including:

- use of official ĀML™ or ĀRU™ logos in product branding;
- official ĀML Compatible™ badges or certification branding;
- use of named conformance-level branding in a manner implying official certification;
- “Powered by ĀML™” commercial co-branding programs;
- hosted or managed AML infrastructure supplied by ĀRU;
- enterprise implementation, architecture, integration, training, or support;
- commercial redistribution that wants official endorsement or certification;
- strategic partnerships involving the official AML roadmap, registries, or certification program;
- OEM or embedded branding arrangements.

## Why the distinction exists

ĀML is intentionally separating:

1. **software freedom and interoperability**, which can accelerate adoption; from
2. **official brand authority, certification, endorsement, and commercial services**, which remain controlled.

A company can implement the open protocol without buying a license to the code. A company that wants to present itself as officially certified, endorsed, partnered, co-branded, or licensed under reserved AML/ĀRU marks should contact the rights holder.

Any such rights must be granted in a separate written agreement by an authorized rights holder.
