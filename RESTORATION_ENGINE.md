# 1. Overview

The AML Restoration Engine evaluates whether a rendered system improves the condition of the human interacting with it.

Traditional systems optimize:
- engagement
- retention
- stimulation
- behavioral extraction

The AML Restoration Engine instead evaluates:
- clarity
- coherence
- comprehension
- cognitive stabilization
- emotional regulation
- restoration of agency

The engine exists to measure whether interaction leaves the user more whole rather than more fragmented.
