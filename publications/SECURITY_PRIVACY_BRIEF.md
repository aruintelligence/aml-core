# Brief for Security and Privacy Teams

**Status: SHIPPED**

ĀML is useful to security/privacy teams because interface decisions can become inspectable artifacts rather than transient pixels.

Relevant surfaces include:

- declared purpose
- data-collection intent
- consent context
- policy decisions
- receipts
- tamper-evident evidence
- detached verification

The key boundary is important: a receipt can prove what was declared and bound together; it cannot prove the declaration was truthful or legally sufficient.

A strong review question becomes:

> Can we independently verify what the interface said it was doing and whether the recorded evidence changed after the fact?
