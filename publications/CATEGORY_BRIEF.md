# Category Brief — Interface Accountability for AI-Generated UI

**Status: PITCH built on SHIPPED AML proof surfaces**

AI systems are moving from generating text to generating interface behavior: prompts, buttons, panels, recommendations, permission requests, checkout flows, and actions.

Traditional frontend controls answer important questions about code, origin, permissions, data, and styling. They do not automatically answer:

> What meaning did the machine declare for this interface element, what policy judged it, and what evidence remains after it rendered or was suppressed?

ĀML proposes a category around that gap: **interface accountability**.

The narrow public spine is:

**AI proposes. ĀML evaluates. Existing UI renders. The receipt explains.**

This is a category proposal, not evidence that the market has already adopted the category or terminology.
