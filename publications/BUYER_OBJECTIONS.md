# Buyer Objections — Answered Without Hype

**Status: SHIPPED**

## “Why do we need this if we already have a design system?”
A design system governs components and consistency. ĀML explores declared meaning, render decisions, and receipts.

## “Why not just use a policy engine?”
You can. ĀML is specifically shaped around the human-interface boundary and can complement a general policy engine.

## “Is this another frontend rewrite?”
No. The prototype includes wrapper, DOM-attribute, HTTP, React, and browser integration paths.

## “Are the scores scientifically validated?”
No. They are declared/model inputs in the current prototype.

## “Does this make us compliant?”
No. It does not guarantee legal, regulatory, or accessibility compliance.

## “Does a receipt prove the AI told the truth?”
No. It proves integrity of the recorded declaration and decision context, not hidden intent.

## “Why should we pilot it?”
Because generated UI creates a review problem that code linting and screenshots do not fully address. A small pilot can tell you whether an explicit interface decision boundary improves your workflow.

## “What is the lowest-risk test?”
One generated UI region. One policy. One receipt. One replay.
