**Status: SHIPPED**

## What is ĀML?
An interface firewall between AI/app intent and pixels.

## What problem does it solve?
It makes declared interface purpose and policy decisions inspectable instead of leaving them implicit in generated UI.

## Does it replace HTML or React?
No.

## What is the prototype rule?
`render_allowed = restoration_value >= attention_cost`

## Are those values objective measurements?
No. They are declared/model inputs in the prototype.

## What is a receipt?
An inspectable record of what was declared and how the runtime decided.

## What is View Meaning?
A way to inspect declared interface meaning and receipt context.

## What is Meaning Gate?
A CI path for detecting semantic/policy regressions before deployment.

## Is ĀML a global standard?
No. It is a working research prototype with public interoperability contracts.

## Is the code open source?
Covered software is MIT licensed.

## Does MIT grant official AML branding rights?
No. Software and official brand rights are separate.

## Can it be piloted without rewriting an app?
Yes. Browser, DOM, HTTP, and framework integration paths exist.

## Does it guarantee legal compliance?
No.

## Does it guarantee accessibility compliance?
No.

## Does it prove AI intent is truthful?
No.

## Does it have independent external witnesses yet?
The public external witness registry remains empty until outside evidence exists.

## Why should a company care now?
Generated interfaces are becoming faster and more dynamic while review systems remain mostly code- and screenshot-centric.
