# ĀML Media / Podcast Brief

**Status: PITCH — ready for interviews and media outreach**

## One sentence
ĀML is an interface firewall between AI/app intent and pixels that makes declared purpose, policy decisions, and receipts inspectable.

## Why now
AI-generated interfaces can change faster than conventional human review processes. ĀML explores a reviewable boundary before those outputs become pixels.

## The 20-second demo
Open:
https://aruintelligence.github.io/aml-core/proof.html?attention=5&restoration=1&lang=en

It says SUPPRESS. Change `restoration_value` from 1 to 5. It becomes ALLOW.

## Three questions worth asking
1. Should generated interfaces be required to declare what they are trying to do?
2. Should a render decision leave a receipt?
3. Should an outside verifier be able to reproduce that decision without trusting the original page?

## What not to say
- ĀML is not a ratified global standard.
- It does not objectively measure human wellbeing.
- It does not guarantee legal or accessibility compliance.
- It does not prove an AI's declared purpose is truthful.
