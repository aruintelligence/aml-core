# Why Teams Might Try ĀML

**Status: SHIPPED**

A team does not need to believe every long-term AML idea to test whether one capability is useful.

## Reason 1 — Generated UI review
Put a narrow generated region behind an explicit decision boundary.

## Reason 2 — Dark-pattern regression checks
Track whether a UI change becomes more attention-demanding or less restorative under the selected policy.

## Reason 3 — Inspectable receipts
Keep a machine-readable record of what was declared and why the interface rendered or was suppressed.

## Reason 4 — View Meaning
Give developers, reviewers, and auditors a way to inspect declared interface intent rather than only final pixels.

## Reason 5 — CI gating
Use Meaning Gate to surface semantic changes in pull requests.

## Reason 6 — Independent verification
Verify public AML witness artifacts in another runtime instead of trusting the reference page.

## Fastest evaluation path

1. Open the proof.
2. Reproduce ALLOW/SUPPRESS.
3. Run the 10-minute local path.
4. Try one existing-HTML integration.
5. Decide whether the receipts improve your review process.
