What is being tested

The current experiment examines repeated high-salience interface elements such as sales/contact calls to action across ribbons, navigation, hero sections, body sections, footers, and mobile action docks. Site-specific phone numbers, SMS routing, and email addresses are preserved as local ground truth.

render_allowed = restoration_value >= attention_cost

Attention/restoration values used in these pilots are declared or model-supplied policy inputs. They are not claimed objective measurements of cognition, psychology, neurological state, wellbeing, morality, or clinical outcome.

Evidence protocol

1. Preserve before state

Checkpoint and source commit before interface changes.

2. Identify the element

Exact UI element, purpose, and placement under test.

3. Preserve provenance

Source and method for labels and declared inputs.

4. Apply policy

Record ALLOW, SUPPRESS, or other explicit outcome.

5. Preserve after state

Before/after evidence and implementation result.

6. Publish receipts

Inspectable evidence without overstating validation.

Evidence boundary

These are project-authored field experiments. They are not independent reproduction, third-party certification, scientific validation, or proof of improved conversion performance. Failures and mixed outcomes remain valid evidence.
