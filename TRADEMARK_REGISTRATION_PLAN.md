## Clearance before filing

Before spending heavily on a filing, search for confusingly similar marks, not just exact matches. Review:

- USPTO trademark search records;
- relevant software/service markets;
- domain names and app/package ecosystems;
- state/common-law use where appropriate.

A trademark attorney can perform a more complete clearance analysis and advise on registrability, likelihood of confusion, ownership, filing basis, and descriptions of goods/services.

## Filing basis

For each listed good/service, confirm either:

- current qualifying use in commerce; or
- a bona fide intent to use the mark for that good/service.

Do not overclaim goods/services that are neither in use nor genuinely intended.

## Important commercial point

Federal trademark registration can strengthen rights in the brand, but it does **not** retroactively convert MIT-licensed code into paid software and does not create a right to charge everyone who implements the open protocol.

The commercial leverage comes from protecting and licensing official brand use, certification, endorsement, managed services, enterprise support, OEM/co-branding, and other separately contracted offerings.
