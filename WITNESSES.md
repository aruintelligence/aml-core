## What counts as a witness

A witness is an external person or organization that publicly does at least one of the following:

- reproduces an ĀML fixture;
- files a bug from a real run;
- publishes an independent writeup;
- ships an integration;
- implements a compatible fixture independently;
- documents an adversarial failure.

Internal ĀRU activity does not count.
