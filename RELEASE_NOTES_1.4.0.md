## Official identity and open-source boundary

The software remains MIT licensed. The official ĀML™ / ĀRU™ names, logos, compatibility branding, certification-style identity, endorsement, OEM/co-branding, and related brand rights remain separate from the software license.

Technical conformance does not automatically imply official authorization or endorsement.

ĀRU can issue scoped, signed `aml-brand-authorization/1` credentials after an appropriate written agreement. Official verification requires both cryptographic validity and a signer fingerprint present in the canonical active ĀRU trust-root registry.

The production private signing key is not published in this repository.

## Evidence boundary

ĀML improves inspectability, policy control, reproducibility, interoperability, and cryptographic integrity. It does not establish that current attention/restoration scores objectively measure cognition or wellbeing, does not make an AI's declared intent truthful, and does not replace WCAG conformance or assistive-technology testing.

Cryptographic signatures prove integrity and key possession. Trust still depends on governance, key custody, revocation, and the underlying agreements or institutions.
