# ĀML FAQ — 20 Questions, Brutal Honesty

**Status: DRAFT**

1. **What is ĀML?**  
ĀML is a prototype interface firewall between AI/app intent and pixels. It makes declared purpose, attention cost, restoration value, policy decisions, and a receipt inspectable.

2. **Is ĀML a replacement for HTML?**  
No. The current implementation can compile to browser-compatible output and can sit in front of existing UI stacks.

3. **Is ĀML a global standard?**  
No. It is a working research prototype with public RFCs, fixtures, and conformance tooling.

4. **What is the prototype rule?**  
`render_allowed = restoration_value >= attention_cost`.

5. **Are attention cost and restoration value scientifically validated measurements?**  
No. They are declared/model inputs in the current prototype.

6. **Does ĀML prove an AI told the truth about its intent?**  
No. ĀML can record and evaluate declared intent; it cannot magically verify hidden motives.

7. **What does a receipt prove?**  
It can prove what data, policy decisions, hashes, and outputs a particular AML execution bound together.

8. **Does a receipt prove an interface is ethical?**  
No. It improves inspectability and reproducibility; moral correctness still depends on policy quality and evidence.

9. **Can ĀML stop a dark pattern?**  
It can suppress an element when its declared properties fail the selected policy. The proof demo shows that mechanism.

10. **Can developers use ĀML without rewriting an app?**  
Yes. The repository includes browser, JavaScript, React, GitHub Action, and HTTP-service paths.

11. **Is there a browser demo?**  
Yes: https://aruintelligence.github.io/aml-core/playground.html

12. **What is View Meaning?**  
A browser surface for inspecting the declared meaning and accountability data associated with an AML execution receipt.

13. **What is Meaning Gate?**  
A CI path for detecting semantic or policy regressions before deployment.

14. **Is the code open source?**  
Yes. Covered software is MIT licensed.

15. **Does MIT also give permission to use official AML/ĀRU branding?**  
No. Software rights and official brand rights are separate.

16. **Does technical conformance mean official certification by ĀRU?**  
No. Technical conformance and official branding/authorization are distinct.

17. **Does ĀML guarantee WCAG compliance?**  
No. Accessibility policies and audits are supplemental and do not replace WCAG or assistive-technology testing.

18. **What would make ĀML more credible?**  
Independent reproductions, external implementations, adversarial review, real integrations, and evidence that the developer experience is simple.

19. **What should a skeptic do first?**  
Open the demo, change `restoration_value`, inspect the decision, and try to reproduce or break it.

20. **What is the shortest honest pitch?**  
ĀML is an interface firewall that makes AI-generated UI decisions inspectable before and after rendering.

Open the demo. Change `restoration_value`. Screenshot the decision and receipt. File it.
