# A badge for proof, not certification

**Status: PITCH**

Repositories can now point to a reproducible AML proof with a small badge:

```md
[![ĀML proof available](https://aruintelligence.github.io/aml-core/proof-badge.svg)](https://aruintelligence.github.io/aml-core/proof.html?attention=5&restoration=1)
```

The wording is deliberately narrow: **PROOF AVAILABLE**.

It does not claim official certification, partnership, endorsement, or trademark authorization.

Badge documentation:
https://github.com/aruintelligence/aml-core/blob/main/docs/PROOF_BADGE.md

Open the proof. Change `restoration_value`. Screenshot the decision and receipt. File it.
