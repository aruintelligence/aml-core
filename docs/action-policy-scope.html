The policy boundary

A rule that allows a tool and destination without approval intentionally accepts varied purposes and arguments. That may be appropriate for a trusted, constrained tool. If content matters, the host must enforce its own content rule or require a fresh decision for the exact proposal digest.

A digest is a binding, not a person's consent. A host must derive the real destination from the arguments, own its policy and tool callbacks, and independently authorize any new digest.
