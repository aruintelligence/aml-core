# 1. Overview

The AML Attention Model defines how AML systems evaluate,
protect,
and preserve human attention.

Traditional software ecosystems frequently optimize for:
- engagement
- retention
- stimulation
- interruption frequency
- compulsive interaction
- behavioral extraction

AML instead treats attention as:
- finite
- biologically expensive
- cognitively limited
- ethically significant
- foundational to human coherence

Attention becomes protected infrastructure.
